Diagnostic Classification Scope

The deep convolutional model is trained to identify and differentiate the following 6 dermatological conditions:

1. Atopic Dermatitis

Chronic pruritic inflammatory skin condition presenting with xerosis, erythematous patches, and flexural lichenification.

2. Contact Dermatitis

Localized acute cutaneous eruption triggered by direct skin contact with exogenous irritants or allergens.

3. Eczema

Broad dermatological spectrum featuring superficial skin inflammation, scaling, microvesicles, and pruritus.

4. Scabies

Contagious parasitic infestation by Sarcoptes scabiei with intense nocturnal pruritus and characteristic burrows.

5. Seborrheic Dermatitis

Inflammatory condition affecting sebum-dense cutaneous areas, displaying greasy scales and erythematous plaques.

6. Tinea Corporis

Superficial dermatophytic fungal infection presenting with sharply demarcated, annular, erythematous scaling lesions.
