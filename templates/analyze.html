Clinical Imaging Protocol

To optimize classification accuracy and avoid false positives:

  • 1
    Adequate Illumination: Use natural or bright diffuse light. Avoid heavy glare or flash reflections directly on the lesion.
  • 2
    Centering & Framing: Ensure the affected region occupies the central 60–80% of the image frame.
  • 3
    Sharp Focus: Tap to autofocus on the skin texture. Blurry or out-of-focus samples degrade model confidence.
  • 4
    Obstruction Removal: Clear hair, bandages, or jewelry from the lesion perimeter before capture.
